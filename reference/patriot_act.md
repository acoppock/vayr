# Original and Replication data for the Patriot Act experiment described in Persuasion in Parallel

Original and Replication data for the Patriot Act experiment described
in Persuasion in Parallel

## Usage

``` r
patriot_act
```

## Format

### `patriot_act`

A tibble with 2062 rows and 4 columns:

- sample_label:

  The original study (Chong and Druckman (2011) or the Mechanical Turk
  replication)

- pid_3:

  Subject partisanship (limited to Republicans and Democracts, including
  leaners)

- T1_content:

  Content of assigned treatment condition: pro-Patriot act statements,
  anti-Patriot act statements, or a control

- PA_support:

  Post-treatment support for the Patriot Act on a 1 to 7 Likert scale
